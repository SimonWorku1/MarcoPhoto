
Assignment 
The goal of this assignment is to conduct two playtests with groups of players who are unfamiliar with your game. They do not have to be strangers to you – but you need to make sure that they are people who a) will give you unbiased feedback on your project and b) have no experience or idea about your game. 
For each playtest you will need a different group of people.   For this assignment you will be graded on your preparation (interview questions and selection of play testers), your writeup and the quality of the feedback received and how you plan to act on it. Overall, I am looking for intentional decision making and critical thinking across your tests. 
You will submit this to Brightspace Assignments 
Stranger Playtest Prep  
Before your playtest you should prepare the following information. This information can be the same across both playtests.
	•	What do you hope to learn from this playtest? 
	•	Several Questions you plan to ask your play testers in their post playtest interview. (You should have at least 4) These questions should align with your overall goals. Some examples: 
	•	What about the game was fun for you? 
	•	What did you struggle the most learning within the game? 
	•	What did you have the least amount of fun with?  
Stranger Playtest Write Up 
After you complete your playtest you will complete a playtest write up. You can use the template below:   Game Title : Stranger Playtest #1
Play Tester Information 
	•	Who is playtesting your game and what (if anything) is their relationship to you? 
	•	Why did you pick them to playtest your game – what about these individuals makes you think you will get valuable feedback (ie: I picked my cousins who are fond of card games because I think their understanding of the genre will give me good insight on my card game)  
Play Test Setup 
	•	Your Prep Questions go here (What do you hope to learn, your questions etc) 
Post Playtest Summary 
	•	What feedback did you receive from your play testers after the playtest (This should include mentions of your questions with your high level takeaways) 
	•	Based on this feedback what do you think you should do next (Note: There is no obligation for you to follow any of the feedback given to you in these playtests, but if that’s the case, you should write up here why you are choosing to ignore it)
